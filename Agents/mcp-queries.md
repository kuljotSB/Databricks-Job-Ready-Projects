## Queries to run on CarbonOps MCP Server

1) <strong>Predict Carbon Emissions</strong> - How much carbon emissions can we expect in June 2026 if our factory consumes 50,000 kWh of electricity, 2,500 litres of diesel, 1,200 m³ of natural gas, and 500 kg of LPG, while producing 10,000 units over 500 operating hours at 32°C?

2) <strong>Predict Emissions Risk</strong> - Our factory's November 2026 electricity consumption is 65,000 kWh, diesel 3,200 litres, natural gas 1,500 m³, and LPG 650 kg. We produced 12,000 units over 550 operating hours at 32°C. Current emissions are 210 tonnes, last month's were 195 tonnes, and our monthly budget is 200 tonnes. Are we at risk of exceeding our emissions budget next month?

3) <strong>Calculate Carbon Intensity</strong> - Our factory produced 25,000 units and emitted 180 tonnes of CO₂e. Our target is 8 kg CO₂e per unit. Are we meeting our sustainability target?

4) <strong>Simulate Emissions Reductions</strong> - Our factory consumed 50,000 kWh of electricity, 2,500 litres of diesel, 1,200 m³ of natural gas, and 500 kg of LPG in June 2026, producing 10,000 units over 500 operating hours at 32°C. How much carbon emissions could we save by reducing electricity consumption by 20% and diesel consumption by 30%?
